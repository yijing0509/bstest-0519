Interests

Drawing

畫畫

從小對於畫畫就有興趣，每天有空閒的時候都會隨筆畫一下，真的非常喜歡。這是我記錄生活與想像的方式！
I've loved drawing since I was a kid. Whenever I have some free time, I'll just sit down and doodle. It's something I genuinely, deeply enjoy.

Fiction Lover

小說愛好者

從國中開始，就發現自己對小說情有獨鍾。特別佩服作者們能透過文字，精準傳達出深刻的情感與意義。除了故事本身，我也迷戀實體書本散發出的淡淡香氣，那種觸感與味道，總能讓我感到無比安心。
I’ve had a special bond with novels since junior high. I’m always in awe of how writers convey profound emotions and meanings through simple words. Beyond the stories themselves, I’m obsessed with the gentle scent of physical books—that touch and aroma always bring me a deep sense of comfort and peace.

Gaming

遊戲

大概從國小開始，我就跟遊戲結下了不解之緣。小時候在鄉下最喜歡和弟弟一起玩主機遊戲；到了國中擁有第一支手機後，便開啟了多采多姿的遊戲世界。因為滿滿的好奇心，我玩的類型非常廣泛，從槍戰、音遊、戀愛、益智到恐怖遊戲都有涉獵。不過，其中最讓我著迷的絕對是「音遊」！那種完全沉浸在音樂節奏中的感覺，真的讓人難以自拔。
My gaming journey started back in elementary school, playing console games with my younger brother in the countryside. Once I got a phone in junior high, a whole new world opened up. Driven by curiosity, I've explored a wide range of genres—from shooters and otome games to puzzles and horror. But my absolute favorite will always be rhythm games; there’s nothing like losing myself completely in the beat.

Music

聽音樂

國小音樂課第一次接觸音樂時，就發現自己挺喜歡的。因為這份熱愛，小時候還當過音樂小老師，搞不好我真的有一點點音樂天賦也說不定？（笑）平常的我是一個超級「雜食性」的聽眾，不管是流行、古風、輕音樂還是各種類型，我的守備範圍超級廣泛——噢對了，除了重金屬音樂之外
My love for music trace back to elementary school music class. I enjoyed it so much that I even became the music class leader—maybe I’ve got a little hidden talent! When it comes to songs, I'm a total omnivore with a super wide music taste. I will literally listen to anything... well, except for heavy metal.
